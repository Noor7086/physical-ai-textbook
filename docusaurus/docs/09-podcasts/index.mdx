# Podcasts

Welcome to the **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Podcast Series** -- a conversational companion to this textbook. Each episode explores key topics from the course in an accessible, discussion-driven format, making complex concepts easier to absorb whether you are commuting, exercising, or reviewing material.

## Available Languages

All episodes are produced in two languages:

- **English** -- Full-length episodes covering each topic in depth.
- **Urdu** -- Complete Urdu-language versions for broader accessibility.

## How to Use These Podcasts

1. **Before reading a chapter** -- Listen to the corresponding episode for a high-level overview.
2. **After completing a chapter** -- Revisit the episode to reinforce key takeaways.
3. **On the go** -- Download episodes for offline listening during commutes or workouts.

Each episode includes timestamps and references to specific sections of the textbook so you can easily cross-reference the material.
